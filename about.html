Our purpose

Every plant has its own needs, and remembering them can be a challenge. Plant Pal brings useful care details together so you can spend less time guessing and more time enjoying the greenery around you.

How Plant Pal helps

Create a simple record for each plant with its name, species, location, and watering frequency. Use the care guides as a starting point for learning how different plants thrive.

Made for every grower

Whether you are caring for your first plant or adding to a growing collection, Plant Pal is designed to make the everyday routine approachable and easy to follow.
