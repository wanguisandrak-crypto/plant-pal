A PRACTICAL STARTING POINT

Plant care, made simpler.

Explore straightforward care notes for popular houseplants. Use them as a guide, then adjust to your plant, pot, and home.

🌵

SUCCULENT

Aloe vera

Light
Bright light; a sunny window is often a good fit.
Water
Let the potting mix dry out completely before watering thoroughly.
Soil
Use a fast-draining cactus or succulent mix and a pot with drainage.

Care tip: Avoid letting the pot sit in water; excess moisture can damage roots.

🌿

TRAILING FOLIAGE

Pothos

Light
Bright, indirect light is ideal; it can also tolerate lower light.
Water
Water when the top of the potting mix feels dry. Reduce watering in cooler, darker months.
Soil
Choose a well-draining indoor potting mix and a pot with drainage.

Care tip: Pothos is toxic if ingested, so keep it away from pets and children.

🪴

DURABLE FOLIAGE

Snake plant

Light
Thrives in bright, indirect light and tolerates lower-light spaces.
Water
Let the potting mix dry out fully between waterings; water less often in winter.
Soil
Use a free-draining mix and a container with drainage holes.

Care tip: Overwatering is a common problem; check the soil before adding water.

🌱

EASY-CARE FAVORITE

Spider plant

Light
Provide bright, indirect light; protect leaves from intense midday sun.
Water
Water when the top layer of soil begins to dry, keeping the mix lightly moist but not soggy.
Soil
A standard, well-draining houseplant mix works well.

Care tip: Brown leaf tips can have several causes, including dry air or minerals in water; check conditions before changing your routine.

Care needs vary with light, temperature, pot size, and the season. Check the soil and your plant’s condition before watering, and make gradual adjustments.
